# 社区建设参与细则

社区建设是指帮助更多普通人顺利学习、参与项目和开展协作的工作，不限于写代码。

## 可以参与什么

- **课程与教程**：共创课程、编写案例、校对内容、录制演示或参与辅导；
- **新人支持**：整理常见问题、帮助新人找到任务、完善入门说明；
- **活动组织**：策划共学、分享、黑客松和线上或线下交流；
- **网站与资料**：建设社区网站，维护首页、资料库、表单和公开文档；
- **推广与合作**：介绍社区项目、制作真实内容、对接合适的合作资源；
- **物资支持**：在事先确认需求后，提供活动或项目会实际使用的场地、设备、奖品、软件、云资源或印刷物料。

## 如何开始

1. 从上面的方向选择一项具体工作；
2. [提交社区建设提议](https://github.com/shengjidaguai-china/.github/issues/new?template=community-building.yml)，说明目标、交付结果、预计时间和需要的协作；
3. 与社区负责人确认范围、公开方式和隐私边界后再开始；
4. 完成后在 Issue 中附上页面、文档、图片、活动记录或其他可以核验的结果。

## 基本约定

- 先确认真实需求，不为了留下记录制造无用工作；
- AI 生成的内容需要经过人工判断、验证和修改；
- 未经同意，不公开群聊、联系方式、照片和其他私人信息；
- 推广内容说明与社区或项目的真实关系，不夸大成果；
- 捐赠物资应先确认用途、接收人和使用方式，未实际需要或无法使用的物资不接收；
- 社区建设记录用于公开协作和致谢，不设置积分或排名。

参与项目代码、设计、测试或文档，请改用[项目参与规则](./CONTRIBUTING.md)。

# Community-building guide

Community building helps everyday people learn, join projects, and collaborate successfully. It is not limited to writing code.

## Ways to contribute

- **Courses and tutorials:** co-create courses, write examples, review content, record demonstrations, or support learners;
- **Newcomer support:** organize common questions, help newcomers find tasks, and improve onboarding materials;
- **Activities:** organize guided learning, talks, hackathons, and online or offline gatherings;
- **Website and resources:** improve the community website, homepage, library, forms, and public documentation;
- **Outreach and partnerships:** introduce real community projects, create accurate content, and connect suitable partners or resources;
- **Material support:** after confirming a real need, provide venues, equipment, prizes, software, cloud resources, or printed materials that an activity or project will actually use.

## How to start

1. Choose one specific piece of work from the areas above;
2. [Submit a community-building proposal](https://github.com/shengjidaguai-china/.github/issues/new?template=community-building.yml) with the goal, deliverable, expected timing, and support needed;
3. Confirm scope, publication, and privacy boundaries with the community organizer before starting;
4. When finished, add a page, document, image, activity record, or another verifiable result to the issue.

## Basic agreements

- Confirm a real need first; do not create work merely to leave a public record;
- AI-generated content must be reviewed, verified, and revised by a person;
- Do not publish group chats, contact details, photos, or other private information without permission;
- Outreach must accurately describe its relationship to the community or project and must not exaggerate results;
- Material donations require an agreed purpose, recipient, and use before acceptance; unneeded or unusable materials will not be accepted;
- Community-building records support public collaboration and acknowledgment; they do not use points or rankings.

For project code, design, testing, or documentation, follow the [project contribution rules](./CONTRIBUTING.md).
